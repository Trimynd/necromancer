PROJECT NECROMANCER - 10 SLIDE SPOOKY DECK
Slide text and image directions

====================================================================

HOW EACH SLIDE IS WRITTEN

TITLE - the big heading
TEXT - the words that go on the slide
IMAGE - what the picture shows, where it sits, how big it is
SAY - what the presenter says out loud

Every image follows the same look:
- Dark gothic, almost black background with drifting fog
- Glowing teal and cyan light as the main colour
- Mode colours: green for Launch, blue for Grow, amber for Rescue,
  violet for Revive, red for the Inquisitor and danger
- Ghosts are see-through, glowing, wearing hooded cloaks with faint
  circuit-line patterns
- No words or letters inside the images
- One side of each image left dark and empty so the slide text can
  sit on top

Fonts: Cinzel Decorative for titles, IBM Plex Sans for text,
IBM Plex Mono for numbers and logs.

TITLE

TEXT
From idea to afterlife.

newborn, thriving, bleeding out, or already buried.

bleeds, and sometimes it dies. We built a necromancer for all of it.
Tell it about your business and it summons an army of ghost agents
to tell you where you stand and what to do next."

TEXT
- Founders bury their savings under beliefs they never tested

- Consultants cost a fortune. Chatbots give everyone the same answer.
"We find the curse before you spend a single rupee."

rests a cracked lightbulb, faintly glowing teal, like the last light

on top, its eyes glowing cyan. Bare twisted trees and a pale moon.
Placement: left 55% of the slide, full height.

Mood: sad, quiet, haunting.

like 'students will pay this price' or 'discounts will bring them

comes from.)

company that's working, the Emergency Exorcism when the numbers are

Live feed (demo data):

Shows: an army of see-through glowing ghosts floating through an

Thin threads of light run from every page to a large crystal orb
floating in the middle, which collects all the knowledge. Many

Placement: right 60% of the slide, full height.
Text position: bullets on the top left; the live feed in a black

Mood: busy, magical, everyone working at once.

"Once raised, the ghosts hunt in parallel. Each one searches the web
for its own mission and writes what it finds onto the Blackboard, a
shared memory all the others can read. Every claim must come with a
source. If we can't show where it came from, we don't say it."

SLIDE 6 - THE SPECTRAL COURTROOM

TITLE
The ghosts argue. The Judge decides.

TEXT
1. Each ghost presents its case: claim, evidence, how sure it is
2. The Judge accepts it, or sends it back: "Bring better proof."
3. Ghosts sent back return to the hunt with the Judge's guidance
4. The Judge announces the verdict

IMAGE (ghosts talking to each other and taking decisions)
Shows: a grand gothic courtroom inside a ruined cathedral. On a high
stone bench sits a huge ghostly Judge in flowing robes with glowing
cyan eyes, raising a gavel made of light. Below, a half-circle of
see-through ghosts argue with each other and present evidence: one
points at a floating glowing chart, one holds up a glowing scroll,
two lean toward each other mid-debate, one raises a hand to object.
Beams of teal light pass between the ghosts as they speak. Broken
stained-glass windows glow faint violet, fog rolls over the floor.
Placement: fills the whole slide.
Text position: the four steps in a see-through dark strip along the
bottom, numbered with glowing cyan circles.
Mood: dramatic, a trial in progress.

SAY
"Then the ghosts stand trial. Each presents its case: what it claims,
its evidence and how sure it is. The Judge, our biggest model, rules
on every case. A weak or off-topic case is sent back with exact
guidance, and that ghost goes back to research. Only then does the
Judge announce the verdict."

SLIDE 7 - THE GRAND INQUISITOR

TITLE
The Grand Inquisitor tries to kill your plan

TEXT
Launch: "Twelve months from now, you are dead. Why?"
Grow: "How would a rival tear you apart?"
Rescue: "Why will the rescue fail too?"
Revive: "Why will it die a second time?"
Fate: Survives - Survives with changes - Perishes

IMAGE
Shows: a frightening ghostly Grand Inquisitor in torn red and black
robes and a tall pointed hood. The face is hidden, only burning red
eyes. A bony finger points straight at the viewer. Behind them, a
storm of red-lit papers and charts being ripped apart by a ghostly
wind. Teal fog curls along the floor.
Placement: left half of the slide, full height. Give the whole slide
a dark red tint.
Text position: the four attacks as four red-bordered cards on the
right half; the fate line underneath.
Mood: threatening, the plan is under attack.
(You can also use frontend/public/assets/inquisitor.jpg.)

SAY
"Before any verdict, the Grand Inquisitor tries to kill your plan, and
the attack changes with the ritual. It drags your riskiest assumption
into the light and tells you the cheapest way to test it."

(If a judge asks: the separate Inquisitor step is designed and shown
in the app, but in today's backend the Judge does this job.)

TEXT

The spirits estimate. Cold arithmetic decides.

Mood: the answer revealed.

SAY
"The ghosts change every time, but the Tome doesn't. A verdict, your

lasts and a 24-month forecast. Our first law: the AI only estimates
the assumptions, plain code does the maths. Drag the EMI partners

SLIDE 9 - THE EXORCISM

TITLE
Case file: a phone shop bleeding out

TEXT
Sales fell from Rs 18L to Rs 9L a month. Rs 12L left in the bank.
What's killing it:
- A rival megastore opened nearby (outside force)
- Buyers drifting to online apps (outside force)
- EMI from only one bank (inside, curable)
- "Discounts will bring them back" (the cursed assumption)
THE ONE RITUAL: sign 3 more EMI partners this week. Stop discounts.
Prophecy: RECOVERABLE
(illustrative demo data)

IMAGE
Shows: a small neighbourhood phone shop on a rainy Indian city street
at night. The neon sign flickers, half the lights are dead. A faint
amber heartbeat line runs across the shop window, sinking toward a
flatline. A see-through ghost floats at the door, holding a lantern
and studying a glowing ledger.
Placement: small, top right corner, about one quarter of the slide.
Also on this slide: a falling amber sales chart with red flag markers
("Feb: rival opens", "Mar: discounts begin") on the left half. Take
it from the app's Rescue report or draw it in PowerPoint.
Text position: the causes as a ranked list on the right under the
image; THE ONE RITUAL in a big glowing amber box across the bottom.
Mood: urgent, but there is a way out.

SAY
"Here's our Rescue example. Two phone shops in Pune, sales cut in
half. The ghosts found the problem wasn't everywhere: buyers who
needed EMI went to a new megastore that offers it through many
banks. Discounts only burned the cash faster. So instead of ten
fixes, we give one: sign three more EMI partners this week and stop
the discounts."
