## Mode 1: Startup Genesis

**Problem:** Most founders build first, validate later. 44% of startups fail from lack of market need . By the time they discover no demand, runway is gone.

**Agent Approach:** Run a **Pre-Mortem** before anything is built . The agents imagine the startup has already failed catastrophically, then work backward to identify why.

**Agent Team (Role Maker generates):**
- **Reality Checker:** Tests the founder's core assumption against real market data
- **Competitor Ghost:** Simulates the incumbent who would crush this startup
- **Unit Economics Analyst:** Calculates whether the math can ever work
- **Pre-Mortem Moderator:** Runs the "future failure" exercise

**How It Thinks:**
1. **Assumption Extraction:** Agent reads the founder's pitch, identifies the *untested assumption* (e.g., "Users will pay ₹500/month for this")
2. **Reality Injection:** Scrapes market data, competitor pricing, customer reviews to test that assumption
3. **Pre-Mortem Simulation:** "It's 12 months from now. The startup is dead. Why?" Agents generate 20+ failure scenarios, categorize them (External, Internal, Assumption Risks) 
4. **Counter-Measure Design:** For the top 3 risks, agents design specific "insurance policies" — concrete actions the founder can take *today* 

**Key Framework:**
- **The "Assumption Debt" Test:** Every startup plan must answer: *What must be true for this to work? What evidence supports that?*
- **SWOT Integration:** Agents conduct competitor SWOT analysis, identifying gaps the startup can exploit 

**Success Metric:** Founder leaves with a **Risk-to-Owner Matrix** — every major risk assigned to a specific counter-measure and owner .

## Mode 2: Competitive Fortress (Preventing Risks in an Operating Company)

**Problem:** Operating companies miss warning signs. CB Insights data shows 72% of failed companies saw their health score decline in the year before death. Partnerships dropped 44% in the final 12 months .

**Agent Approach:** Continuous monitoring + competitive intelligence. The agents become a **Threat Detection System**.

**Agent Team:**
- **Competitor Watcher:** Tracks competitor moves, pricing changes, product launches
- **Customer Sentiment Analyst:** Scrapes reviews, social mentions, support tickets for early churn signals
- **Financial Health Monitor:** Tracks burn rate, CAC trends, LTV deterioration
- **Red Team Agent:** Actively tries to destroy the company's business model

**How It Thinks:**
1. **Competitive Intelligence Gathering:** Uses tools like Moz, Ahrefs, Google Trends to build competitor profiles 
2. **SWOT Comparison:** Compares your company's SWOT against each competitor's SWOT 
3. **Customer Review Analysis:** NLP-powered sentiment analysis on competitor reviews to find their weaknesses 
4. **"Kill the Company" Simulation:** Red Team agent asks: *"If I were a new entrant, how would I destroy this company?"*

**Key Framework:**
- **Benchmark Tracking:** Monitor CLV, CAC, NPS, sales revenue against industry competitors 
- **Partnership Health Score:** Track relationship activity (partnership fall-off is a leading indicator) 
- **Mosaic Score Equivalent:** Build a simple internal health score that trends over time

**Success Metric:** A **live risk dashboard** showing competitive threats, customer sentiment trends, and early warning indicators.

## Mode 3: Turnaround Surgeon (Improving a Downfalling Company)

**Problem:** Angel-funded startups face 70% failure rate, but early intervention boosts survival by 45-60% . Most founders avoid the hard decisions until it's too late .

**Agent Approach:** The agents become a **Turnaround Advisory Board** that forces the founder to confront reality.

**Agent Team:**
- **Forensic Accountant:** Analyzes cash flow, burn rate, unit economics with brutal honesty
- **Customer Truth-Teller:** Interviews customers to find why they really left
- **Team Auditor:** Assesses founder conflicts, dead weight, culture issues
- **Hard Decision Enforcer:** Forces the cuts the founder is avoiding

**How It Thinks:**
1. **Diagnostic Assessment:** Deep dive into financials, operations, and market fit to pinpoint root causes. 42% of failures stem from no market need 
2. **Cash Flow Ruthlessness:** Identify ONE thing that makes or saves money. Everything else pauses 
3. **Pivot Testing:** 75% of successful venture-backed startups underwent at least one major pivot 
4. **Team Surgery:** Address people issues — 20% of startups fail from team problems 

**Key Framework (from EcoGlow Turnaround) :**
- **Reconnect with Core Customer:** Stop trying to please everyone. Find the one segment that loves you
- **Profit Over Vanity:** Cut ad spend, shift to influencer collabs, renegotiate suppliers
- **Reset Culture:** Weekly "war room" sessions, transparent communication
- **D2C Shift:** If distribution is broken, go direct

**The One-Day Reset Protocol :**
1. Kill the noise. No meetings, no decks. Just facts
2. Look at cash first. Real runway, real burn
3. Identify ONE customer, ONE use case, ONE channel
4. Cut anything that doesn't acquire, retain, or serve customers this quarter
5. Talk to real customers. Today

**Success Metric:** A **90-Day Turnaround Plan** with weekly milestones, cash preservation targets, and a single revenue-generating focus.

## Mode 4: Resurrection Engine (Dead Company Revival)

**Problem:** Dead companies leave behind assets (code, customers, brand, domain) and lessons. But reviving them requires understanding *why* they died.

**Agent Approach:** The agents become **Digital Necromancers**. They scrape the corpse, perform an autopsy, and build a zombie.

**Agent Team (Role Maker generates based on cause of death):**
- **Coroner:** Determines cause of death from archives
- **Asset Extractor:** Identifies recoverable code, customers, brand equity
- **Ghost CEO:** Pitches the revival to investors
- **Compliance Ghost:** Ensures the zombie doesn't repeat the original's regulatory mistakes

**How It Thinks:**
1. **Digital Autopsy:** Scrape Wayback, GitHub, HN, Product Hunt for remnants
2. **Cause of Death Analysis:** Categorize into PMF failure (43%), timing (29%), unit economics (19%), or regulatory 
3. **Assumption Debt Recovery:** Extract the *untested assumption* the original died on. The zombie MUST test this first
4. **Regulatory Pre-Flight:** For Indian fintech specifically, check RBI FREE-AI principles (Trust, People First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness, Accountability, Understandable by Design, Safety) 

**Key Framework:**
- **Assumption Debt Auditor:** Every pivot must first answer: *What did the original get wrong? How will we know before we spend?*
- **Compliance Gatekeeper:** For fintech, regulation is a moat, not a checkbox. Paytm Payments Bank died from persistent non-compliance . Simpl was halted by RBI . The zombie must pass compliance BEFORE building

**Success Metric:** A **Zombie MVP** deployed live, with a pitch that explicitly states the original assumption, how it will be tested, and the regulatory path.

## Recommendation: Start with One Mode

For a hackathon, **don't build all four**. Pick the mode that best demonstrates Gemma 4's agentic capabilities:

- **Genesis Mode** shows reasoning + pre-mortem
- **Resurrection Mode** shows scraping + deployment + drama
- **Surgeon Mode** shows hard decision-making

**My recommendation:** Build **Resurrection Mode** as the flagship demo, but architect the code so **Genesis** and **Surgeon** share the same base agents (Role Maker, Pre-Mortem, Compliance Checker). This gives you a demo that looks complete and a system that's extensible.

### 🔍 Key Lessons for Your Necromancer Project

These failures validate the core assumptions behind your handwritten plan, particularly the focus on **Prerequisites**, **Failure**, and **ROI**.

1.  **Assumption Debt is the Silent Killer**: A staggering **42% of AI startups fail because nobody tested the core assumption** that the market needed what they built . Your **"Failure (Pros/Cons)"** agent must extract and force the zombie to test the *untested assumption* (e.g., "Users will share bank data," "Procurement will take 6 months") before spending a single "soul token."
2.  **Regulatory is a Moat, Not a Prerequisite**: In Indian fintech, **RBI compliance is the difference between life and death**. The survivors (like Slice and Jupiter) treated regulation as a **moat**, while the dead (Simpl, Paytm Payments Bank, ZestMoney) treated it as a checkbox . Your **"Prerequisites"** agent must have a hard-coded **Compliance Gatekeeper** that blocks the CTO agent from building until the legal path is clear.
3.  **Scaling Without Foundations is Fatal**: Premature scaling—expanding before unit economics (LTV/CAC) are proven—is a recurring theme. Companies like WeWork and Homejoy collapsed because growth accelerated fragility, not value . Your **"ROI"** and **"Growth"** agents must enforce the **Viability Score Formula** (Market * 0.30 + Risk_Pivot * 0.25, etc.) to prevent the zombie from running out of runway.
4.  **The "Wrapper" Trap**: Startups like Relay died because they were thin wrappers around capabilities that foundation models eventually absorbed . Your **"Competition"** agent (the Competitor Ghost) must ask: *"What happens if OpenAI ships this natively next quarter?"* If the zombie has no defensible moat, it fails the round.

By feeding these specific historical examples into your Role Maker's context, the Necromancer AI won't just generate generic ghost employees—it will spawn a **Compliance Ghost** with a bias toward RBI FREE-AI principles and a **ROI Ghost** obsessed with LTV/CAC, directly addressing the real reasons why fintech startups in your target market die.
