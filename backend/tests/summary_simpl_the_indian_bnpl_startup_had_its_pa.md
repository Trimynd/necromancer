## 2. Question & Context
**Question:** Why did Simpl die, should it be revived, and how? List the competitors and market players.

**Context:** Simpl, an Indian Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) startup, faced a dual-pronged collapse. First, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) halted its payment operations due to unauthorized activity under the Payments and Settlement Systems (PSS) Act. Second, the Enforcement Directorate (ED) initiated a case regarding alleged violations of the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) involving significant capital flows.

## 7. Recommended Next Steps

1.  **Legal Resolution:** Resolve the alleged FEMA discrepancies and the PSS Act non-compliance through formal regulatory channels.
2.  **Structural Pivot:** Transition from a "Payment System Operator" model to a "Lending-led" model by partnering with Regulated Entities (REs) or obtaining an NBFC license.
3.  **Product Re-engineering:** Integrate UPI-based settlement and reward structures to regain the "one-tap" consumer preference lost during the shutdown.
4.  **Compliance-First Growth:** Prioritize regulatory architecture over rapid user acquisition to prevent a secondary collapse.
